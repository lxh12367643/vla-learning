# 时序差分

## 1.TD introduction

### $v_{t+1}(s_t) = v_t(s_t) - \alpha_t(s_t)[v_t(s_t) - [r_{t+1} + \gamma v_t(s_{t+1})]]$

$v_{t+1}(s_t)$:new estimate ,

$v_t(s_t)$:current estimate,

$\bar{v_t} = r_{t+1} + \gamma v_t(s_{t+1})$ is called the ==TD target==.

$\delta_t = v_t(s_t) - \bar{v_t}$ is called  the ==TD error==.



### First ,why is $\bar{v}$ called the TD target ?

- That is because the algorithm drives $v(s_t)$ towards $\bar{v_t}$ .

The algorithm can  be write like this:

​          $\vert v_{t+1}(s_{t}) - \bar{v_t}\vert = \vert 1 - \alpha_t(s_t)\vert \vert v_t(s_t) - \bar{v_t} \vert$

Since $\alpha_t(s_t)$ is a small positive number , we have

​             $0 < 1 - \alpha_t(s_t) <1$

Therefore,

​            $\vert v_{t+1}(s_t) - \bar{v_t}\vert \leq \vert v_t(s_t) - \bar{v_t}\vert$

So , $v_t$ is driven towards $\bar{v_t}$



### Second , what is the interpretation of the TD error?

$\delta_t$ shows the deficiency between $v_t$ and $v_\pi $

we can replace $t$ to the $\pi , t$,then we can find the $\delta_{\pi , t} = 0$

Hence , if $\delta_t \neq 0$ , then $v_t$ is not equal to $v_\pi$.

## 2.Sarsa

`The TD algorithm can only estimate state values `

`But Sarsa can directly estimate action values`

​           The name comes from:  

​                      ($S_t , A_t , R_{t+1} , S_{t+1} , A_{t+1}$) , State-Action-Reward-State-Action

### $Q(S_t , A_t) \leftarrow Q(S_t , A_t) + \alpha[R_{t+1} + \gamma Q(S_{t+1} , A_{t+1}) - Q(S_t , A_t)]$



### - Policy searching by Sarsa

 for each episode , do: 

​		if the current $s_t$ is not the target state , do:

​				collect the experience ($s_t , a_t , r_{t+1} , s_{t+1} , a_{t+1}$):in particular , take action $a_t$ following $\pi_t{s_t}$ , generate $r_{t+1} , s_{t+1}$ , and then take action $a_{t+1}$ following $\pi_t{s_{t+1}}$.

​				update q-value:

​						$q_{t+1}(s_t , a_t) = q_t(s_t , a_t) -      \alpha_t(s_t , a_t)[q_t(s_t , a_t) - [r_{t+1}  +  \gamma q_t(s_{t+1} , a_{t+1})]]$

​                update policy:

$\pi_{t+1}(a|s_t) = 1 - \frac {\epsilon}{\Alpha}(\vert \Alpha\vert - 1) $          if $a = arg \max_a q_{t+1}(s_t , a)$

$\pi_{t+1}(a|s_t) = \frac {\epsilon}{\Alpha}$                                  otherwise

## 3.Expected Sarsa and n-step Sarsa

###  1. expected Sarsa

### $Q(S_t , A_t) \leftarrow Q(S_t , A_t) + \alpha [R_{t+1} + \gamma \sum_a \pi a|S_{t+1}Q(S_{t+1} , a) - Q(S_t , A_t)]$

==$\sum_a \pi (a|S_{t+1})Q(S_{t+1} , A_t)$ is the expectation that choose Q’s value in the strategy $\pi$ (under $S_{t+1}$)==



### 2. n-step Sarsa

### $G_t^{(n)} = R_{t+1} + \gamma R_{t+2} + \dots +\gamma^{n-1}R_{t+n} + \gamma^n Q(S_{t+n} , A_{t+n})$

 then        $Q(S_t , A_t) \leftarrow Q(S_t , A_t) + \alpha[G_t^{(n)} - Q(S_t , A_t)]$

-  $R_{t+1} , \dots , R_{t+n}:$ the real rewards in the following n-step
- $S_{t+n} , A_{t+n}:$ the state and action after n steps
- $A_{t+n}:$ the sample from the current policy

==if n=1 , the algorithm is equal to Sarsa . if $n=\infty$ , the algorithm is equal to MC==

## 4.Q-learning

### $q_{t+1}(s_t , a_t) = q_t(s_t , a_t) - \alpha(s_t , a_t)[q_t(s_t , a_t) - [r_{t+1} + \gamma max_{a\in\Alpha}q_t(s_{t+1} , a)]]$

### $q_{t+1}(s , a) = q_t(s , a) , \forall (s , a) \neq (s_t , a_t)$



### What does Q-learning do mathematically?

It aims to solve

​                       $q(s , a) = E[R_{t+1} + \gamma max_a q(S_{t+1} , a) | S_t = s , A_t = a] , \forall s , a$



### -Policy searching by Q-learning(On-policy version)

​		for each episode , do:

​				if the current $s_t$ is not the target state , do:

​						collect the experience ($s_t , a_t , r_{t+1} , s_{t+1}$): in particular , take 						action $a_t$ following $\pi_t(s_t)$ , generate $r_{t+1} , s_{t+1}$.

​						update q-value:

$q_{t+1}(s_t , a_t) = q_t(s_t , a_t) - \alpha_t(s_t , a_t)[q_t(s_t , a_t) - [r_{t+1} + \gamma max_a q_t(s_{t+1} , a)]]$

​						update policy:

$\pi_{t+1}(a | s_t) = 1 - \frac{\epsilon}{\vert\Alpha\vert}(\vert \Alpha\vert - 1) $        if $a = arg max_a q_{t+1}(s_t , a)$

$\pi_{t+1}(a | s_t) = \frac{\epsilon}{\vert\Alpha\vert}$                                otherwise



### - Optimal policy search by Q-learning(Off-policy version)

​		for each episode${s_0 , a_0 , r_1 , a_1 , r_2 , \dots}$ generated by $\pi_b$ , do:

​				for each step $t = 0 , 1 , 2 , \dots$ of the episode , do:

​						update q-value:

$q_{t+1}(s_t , a_t) = q_t(s_t , a_t) - \alpha (s_t , a_t)[q(s_t , a_t) - [r_{t+1} + \gamma max_a q_t(s_{t+1} , a)]]$

​						update target policy:

$\pi_{T , t+1}(a | s_t) = 1$           if  $a = arg max_a q_{t+1}(s_t , a)$

$\pi_{T , t+1}(a|s_t) = 0$           otherwise

## * On-policy vs Off-policy

### There exist two policy in a TD learning task:

#### - The behavior policy is used to generate experience samples.

#### -The target policy is constantly updated toward an optimal policy. 

- When the behavior policy is the same as the target policy , such kind of learning is called on-policy.
- When they are different , the learning is called off-policy.
